
# TravelGuide homepage — photographic source credits

Updated: 15 November 2026  
Target branch: development/homepage

The 12 new Round 2 derivatives were created from original JPEG downloads. Each source returned HTTP 200 and image/jpeg and was decoded at native dimensions; original and derivative SHA-256 values are recorded in HOMEPAGE-ASSET-MAPPING.md. Output WebP files were cropped proportionally (not stretched), encoded at quality 85 and verified for image/webp MIME, RIFF/WEBP signature and exact target dimensions.

## User-authorised reuse

alpine-hiking.webp is used in both Alpine Hiking and Swiss Trail because it shows the same Matterhorn trail and hikers. This is an explicitly authorised reuse between closely related cards. The legacy Amalfi and Kyoto repeated-image groups remain separately documented; those original files are AI-generated artwork, not Round 2 photographs.
